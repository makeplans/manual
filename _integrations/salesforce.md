# Salesforce integration

With Salesforce connected, MakePlans adds a new contact in Salesforce when a new reservation is made.

## Connect Salesforce

1. Open setup and select **Integrations**, then **Salesforce**.
2. Select **Connect to service**, log in to Salesforce and allow MakePlans to access your account.
3. Back in MakePlans, choose the MakePlans account to connect.
