# Klaviyo integration

With Klaviyo connected, a customer who makes a reservation is added to your Klaviyo list as a subscriber.

## Connect Klaviyo

1. Open setup and select **Integrations**, then **Klaviyo**.
2. Select **Connect to service**. This takes you to Klaviyo.
3. Log in to Klaviyo and allow MakePlans to access your account. If you have several Klaviyo accounts, choose the one to connect.
4. Back in MakePlans, choose the MakePlans account to connect.
5. Select the list new subscribers should be added to. "This service is connected." is shown next to the list you chose.

## Good to know

- Only customers created after you connect are added. To add your existing customers, export them in setup under **Export** and import the file into Klaviyo.
- To let customers choose whether they want marketing, add the consent field to your reservation form. See [Custom fields]({% link _services/custom-fields.md %}).

## Disconnect

Open the integration page again and delete the connection.
