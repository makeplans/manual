# More integrations

These integrations are connected in setup under **Integrations**. Contact support if you need help with any of them.

## Newsletters

| Integration | To connect |
|---|---|
| **MailMojo** | Enter your **Account username** and **List-ID** and select **Save integration**. |
| **Sendy** | Enter the details of your Sendy installation and select **Save integration**. |

## Video meetings

| Integration | To connect |
|---|---|
| **Whereby** | Enter your Whereby API key and select **Save integration**. Use the merge field {% raw %}`{{ booking.whereby_url }}`{% endraw %} to send the meeting link. See [Merge fields](/notifications/merge-fields/). |

## Customer and work tools

| Integration | To connect |
|---|---|
| **Notion** | Select **Connect to service** and log in to Notion. |
| **Highrise** | Select **Connect to service** and log in to Highrise. |

## Messaging

| Integration | To connect |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | Send confirmations and reminders on WhatsApp and chat with your customers. You need a Meta business account and a phone number that is not already used with WhatsApp. Select **Connect WhatsApp** and sign in with Meta. Message texts must be approved by Meta. Contact support to get started. |

## Access control

| Integration | To connect |
|---|---|
| **Unloc** | Connects your digital door locks. Enter the **Client ID** and **Client secret** from Unloc Control Center and select **Save integration**. |

## Industry systems

| Integration | To connect |
|---|---|
| **BestRx** | Select **Enable**. |
| **Extensor** | Requires Extensor Online Booking. Select **Start using the Extensor Online Booking integration**. Extensor will contact you. |
