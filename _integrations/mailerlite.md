# MailerLite integration

With MailerLite connected, a customer who makes a reservation is added to your MailerLite group as a subscriber.

## Connect MailerLite

1. Open setup and select **Integrations**, then **MailerLite**.
2. Enter your **API-key** from MailerLite and select **Save integration**.
3. Select the group new subscribers should be added to. "This service is connected." is shown next to the group you chose.

## Good to know

Only customers created after you connect are added. To add your existing customers, export them in setup under **Export** and import the file into MailerLite.
