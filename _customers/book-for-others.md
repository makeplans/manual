# Book for someone else

Your profile belongs to you: it is tied to your email address and mobile number. You cannot log in as yourself and type another person's name in the form. Instead, add the other person to your profile. The reservation is then made in their name.

1. Start booking as usual and confirm who you are with your own email address or mobile number.
2. On the last step, select **Book for someone else**.
3. Enter the other person's name and details and save.
4. The booking now shows that it is for the other person. Complete it as usual.

Next time, the people you have added are saved. Select **Change** next to the name to pick one of them, or to add another person.
