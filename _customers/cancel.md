# Cancel or change a reservation

Whether you can cancel or change a reservation yourself, and how late, is decided by the business you booked with.

## Cancel

1. Open the link in your confirmation, or log in to the booking site and open the reservation under **My reservations**.
2. Select **Cancel reservation**.

If you do not see a cancel link, the reservation can no longer be cancelled online, for example because it is too close to the start or because it is paid in advance. Contact the business instead.

## Change the time

On the page of your reservation, select **Reschedule** and choose a new time.

## Questions about refunds

Refunds are handled by the business you booked with, not by MakePlans.
