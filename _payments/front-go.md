# Accept payments with Front Go

Front Go is a payment provider for Norway. You need your own agreement with Front Go.

## Connect Front Go

1. Open setup and select **Integrations**, then **Front Go**.
2. Enter the **API key** from your Front Go account.
3. Select **Save integration**.

## Require pre-payment for a service or event

Edit the service or event, enter a price and tick **Pre-payment required**. Customers must then pay to complete a reservation for it.
