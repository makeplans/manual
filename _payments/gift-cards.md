# Gift cards

Customers can buy a gift card and use it to pay for reservations.

## Sell gift cards on the booking site

1. Open setup and select **Products**, then **Add**, then **New gift card**.
2. Enter a **Title**, such as "Gift card 50".
3. Enter the **Price including taxes**. This is the value of the gift card.
4. Add a **Description** if you want one.
5. Save.

Create one for each value you want to offer. Clear **Show on the booking site** to stop selling one.

## Issue a gift card yourself

Use this for a gift card you sell at the counter or give away.

1. Open **Orders** in the main menu and select **Gift cards**, then **New gift card**.
2. Select the **Customer**.
3. Enter the **Initial value**. The **Current value** is what is left on the card.
4. Optionally, set **Valid until**.
5. Select **Save gift card**.

## How customers pay with a gift card

When paying, the customer can select a gift card that is on their profile, or enter a gift card code. A gift card you issue to a customer yourself is added to that customer's profile.

## Keep track

**Orders**, then **Gift cards**, lists every gift card with its value. Open one to change the value, extend the date or clear **Active** to block it.

To export the list, open setup and select **Export**, then **Gift cards**.
