# Create your account

Signing up takes a few minutes. You get a 14 day free trial and do not need a credit card. A wizard then helps you add your first services and resources, and your booking site is live when it finishes.

## Sign up

1. Go to [app.makeplans.com](https://app.makeplans.com) and enter your **Company name**. Customers see this name on the booking site.
2. Check the address shown under **Your booking site**. It is made from your company name. Select **Change** to pick a different one.
3. Check the country and timezone shown below. Select **Change** if they are wrong. Opening hours and reservation times use this timezone.
4. Under **Your user login**, enter your **Name**, **Work email** and a **Password**. This login is personal. You can add more users later.
5. Select **Create my booking site**.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/create-account/01-sign-up-form.webp" alt="The sign-up form with company name, country, timezone and user login filled in" %}

## Choose what customers book

The wizard asks what kind of services you offer.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/create-account/02-wizard-choose-type.webp" alt="The wizard step with two choices: Appointments and Attendance" %}

- **Appointments**: services with a fixed duration that customers book inside your opening hours, such as a consultation, haircut, massage or rental.
- **Attendance**: events, courses and classes that happen at specific times, once or repeating.

Pick the one that fits most of what you do. You can add the other kind later. The rest of this page follows **Appointments**.

{% include alert.html type="tip" content="Select **Manual setup** in the top right corner to skip the wizard and set everything up yourself." %}

## Add your services

1. Enter a **Title** and a **Duration** in minutes for your first service.
2. Select **Add another** for each additional service.
3. Select **Continue**.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/create-account/03-wizard-add-services.webp" alt="The wizard step for adding services, with two services filled in" %}

Title and duration are enough for now. Price and other settings come later.

## Add your resources

A resource is an employee, a room or an object that can be booked.

1. Enter a **Title**, such as the employee's name.
2. Select **Add another** for each additional resource.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/create-account/04-wizard-add-resources.webp" alt="The wizard step for adding resources, with two resources filled in" %}

3. Select **Availability** next to a resource to see when it can be booked. New resources are open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00. Change the days and times if needed and select **Save**.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/create-account/05-wizard-availability.webp" alt="The availability for a resource, open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00" %}

4. Select **Done!**

## Your booking site is live

The last page confirms that your booking site is live and shows its address. You also receive an email with the address. Select **Take me to the calendar** to open the admin.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/create-account/06-wizard-completed.webp" alt="The final wizard page saying the booking site is now live" %}

Every resource you added offers every service you added. You can change this, and everything else from the wizard, at any time.
