# Getting started

This section takes you from a new account to a booking site your customers can use. Follow the pages in order. Most accounts are ready in under half an hour.

1. [What is MakePlans]({% link _getting_started/what-is-makeplans.md %}): the few terms you need to know.
2. [Create your account]({% link _getting_started/create-account.md %}): sign up and run the setup wizard.
3. [Find your way around]({% link _getting_started/find-your-way-around.md %}): the main menu and the setup menu.
4. [Set your opening hours]({% link _getting_started/opening-hours.md %}): when each resource can be booked.
5. [Adjust your services]({% link _getting_started/services.md %}): duration, price and who offers what.
6. [Make a test reservation]({% link _getting_started/test-reservation.md %}): try it as yourself and as a customer.
7. [Share your booking site]({% link _getting_started/share-booking-site.md %}): link, button and QR code.
8. [Activate your account]({% link _getting_started/activate-account.md %}): what the trial includes and how to continue after it.
9. [Next steps]({% link _getting_started/next-steps.md %}): notifications, payments and more users.
