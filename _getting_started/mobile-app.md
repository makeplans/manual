# Use MakePlans as an app on your phone

MakePlans is not in the App Store or on Google Play. It is a web app: it runs in the browser, on any phone, tablet or computer. Add it to your home screen and it gets its own icon and opens in its own window, like the other apps on your phone.

## What a web app is

A web app is built on the open standards of the web instead of being made separately for iPhone and for Android. For you that means:

- **Nothing to download or update.** You always have the latest version, on every device.
- **It works the same everywhere.** iPhone, Android, tablet, Mac and PC all show the same MakePlans, with the same features.
- **You are not tied to one kind of device.** Change phone or borrow a computer, log in, and everything is there.
- **It takes up almost no space** on your phone.

Once it is on your home screen, MakePlans opens full screen without the browser's address bar, and shows up as its own app when you switch between apps.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open Safari and log in to MakePlans, so you see your calendar.
2. Tap the **Share** button, the square with an arrow pointing up. On newer versions of iOS it can be behind the **•••** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. If you see **Open as Web App**, leave it on.
5. Tap **Add**.

This also works from Chrome, Edge and Firefox on iPhone. Use the browser's share button in the same way.

## Android

1. Open Chrome and log in to MakePlans, so you see your calendar.
2. Tap the menu with three dots in the top right corner.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.

MakePlans is now on your home screen and in your list of apps.

Other browsers have the same option under a slightly different name:

| Browser | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| **Samsung Internet** | The menu, then **Add to**, then **Home screen** |
| **Firefox** | The menu with three dots, then **Add app to Home screen** |
| **Edge** | The menu, then **Add to phone** |

The exact wording changes a little between versions. Look for "Install" or "Add to Home screen" in the browser's menu.

## On your computer

You can do the same on a Mac or PC, to get MakePlans in its own window with an icon in the Dock or taskbar.

- **Chrome and Edge:** select the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and select **Install**.
- **Safari on Mac:** select **File**, then **Add to Dock**.

## Good to know

- **Log in once more.** The first time you open MakePlans from the new icon, you may have to log in again.
- **You need an internet connection.** MakePlans does not work offline.
- **There are no push notifications.** To be told about new reservations on your phone, turn on notifications by email or SMS. See [Notifications to you and your staff]({% link _notifications/user-notifications.md %}).
- **Dark mode.** The admin can follow the light or dark setting of your phone. Choose the theme in your [profile]({% link _account/login.md %}).
- **To remove it,** delete the icon the same way as any other app. Nothing in your account is deleted.

## Your reservations in the phone's calendar

To see your reservations in the calendar app on your phone, next to your private appointments, use an iCalendar feed. See [Show reservations in other calendars]({% link _integrations/calendars.md %}).

## For your customers

Your booking site is also a web app. Customers book in the browser and do not download anything. A customer who books often can add your booking site to their home screen in the same way as above.
