# Find your way around

The admin has two levels: a main menu for daily work, and a setup menu for everything you configure.

## The main menu

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/find-your-way-around/01-calendar.webp" alt="The calendar in week view with the main menu across the top" %}

| Menu item | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Agenda** | See reservations as a list, by day, week or month. Filter by resource, service and status, or see the latest changes and newest reservations. |
| **Calendar** | See reservations in a calendar and add new ones. Each resource has its own colour. |
| **Customers** | Search your customers, open a customer to see their reservations, and add new customers. |
| **Orders** | See orders, passes, subscriptions, gift cards and coupons. |
| **Reports** | See figures for reservations, revenue, resources, services, no-shows and more. |

On the right are two icons:

- The **cog** opens the setup menu.
- Your **initials** open the user menu, with **Profile** and **Logout**. In your profile you change your password and can turn on 2-step verification.

## The setup menu

Select the cog to open setup. The menu on the left lists everything you can configure.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/getting-started/find-your-way-around/02-setup-menu.webp" alt="The setup page with the setup menu on the left" %}

These are the ones you will use first:

| Setup page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Resources** | Employees, rooms or objects, and when each can be booked. |
| **Services** | What customers book as appointments. |
| **Event types** | Classes, courses and events customers sign up for. |
| **Booking site** | Design, forms, rules and texts on your booking site. |
| **Promote** | The link, button and QR code for your booking site. |
| **Notifications - customer** | Which emails and SMS your customers receive. |
| **Account** | Company name, address of the booking site, language, currency and timezone. |
| **Users** | Logins for your colleagues. |
| **Billing** | Activate your account and manage your subscription. |

The other pages are covered in their own sections of this help site.
