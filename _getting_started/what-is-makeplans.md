# What is MakePlans

MakePlans gives you two things: a **booking site** where your customers make reservations, and an admin where you see and manage those reservations in a calendar.

To set it up you describe what can be booked and who or what delivers it. MakePlans uses these terms.

## Resources

A resource is an employee, a room or an object. Each reservation belongs to one resource. For a clinic the resources are usually the therapists. For a rental business they are the things you rent out, such as "Small meeting room" or "Projector #4".

Each resource has its own opening hours, so a part-time employee can be bookable on fewer days than a full-time one.

## Services

A service is something customers book as an appointment. It has a fixed duration, such as 30 minutes, and can be booked at any free time inside the opening hours of the resources that offer it. Examples are a consultation, a haircut, a massage or a rental.

## Event types and events

An event type is for attendance at a specific time: a class, a course or a one-off event. You create the event type once (for example "Morning yoga") and then add events to the calendar for the dates it runs, either once or repeating, like every Thursday. Several people sign up for the same event until it is full.

Use services when the customer chooses the time. Use event types when you choose the time and customers sign up for it. You can use both in the same account.

## Reservations

A reservation is one booking in your calendar: a customer, a service or event, a resource and a time. Customers make reservations on the booking site. You can also add them yourself in the admin, for example when someone calls.

## The booking site

Your booking site is a public page with its own address. Customers pick a service, choose a free time, confirm who they are and complete the reservation. Everything they can pick comes from the resources, services and events you have set up.
