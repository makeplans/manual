# Activate your account

A new account is a trial for 14 days. During the trial your booking site works and customers can make reservations. A banner at the bottom of the admin shows how many days are left.

## What is locked during the trial

These setup pages open only after you activate:

- **Communication**: the texts in the standard emails and SMS.
- **Message templates**: extra confirmations, reminders and follow-ups.
- **Notifications - user**: who in your business is notified about new reservations.

If you open one of them during the trial, MakePlans tells you the feature requires an activated account.

## Activate

1. Select the trial banner at the bottom of the page, or select **Billing** in setup.
2. Select **Activate account - Enter credit card details**.
3. Enter your card details.

The **Billing** page shows what you will be charged each month, based on your current usage.
