# Booking site

Your booking site is the public page where customers make reservations. Its settings are in setup under **Booking site**, split into five pages.

| Page | What you set there |
|---|---|
| **Design** | Theme, calendar style, logo and custom CSS |
| **Forms** | The reservation form and the customer registration form |
| **General** | Whether the site is public, language, verification method, address and tracking |
| **Rules** | Approval, waitlist, booking limits, cancellation and terms |
| **Texts** | Footer and the messages customers see |
