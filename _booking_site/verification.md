# Verification

Before a reservation is completed, the customer confirms who they are. This keeps your customer list accurate and stops others from booking in someone else's name.

## Choose the method

1. Open setup and select **Booking site**, then **General**.
2. Choose a **Verification method for new reservations**.
3. Optionally, tick **Additional methods** to let customers choose between several.
4. Select **Save**.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/booking-site/verification/01-verification-method.webp" alt="The verification method menu set to Email, with SMS, ID-porten and Vipps as additional methods" %}

| Method | How the customer verifies |
|---|---|
| **Email** | A code is sent to their email address. |
| **SMS (Recommended)** | A code is sent to their mobile phone. Most customers find this quicker than looking for an email. Uses your SMS credits. |
| **ID-porten (BankID)** | They log in with Norwegian electronic ID. |
| **Vipps** | They log in with Vipps. |

## What the customer does

With email or SMS, the customer enters their email address or phone number, receives a code and types it in. A customer who ticks **Remember me on this machine** does not have to verify again on their next visit from the same device.

New customers are then asked for their name and contact details. Returning customers go straight to confirming the reservation.

## Reservations that are not completed

While a customer is completing a reservation, the time is held for them for 20 minutes. This also applies while they are paying. If they have not finished by then, someone else can book the time.

A customer who finishes later still gets the reservation, as long as nobody else has taken the time in the meantime.

Contact support if you need a different holding time than 20 minutes.
