# Terms and conditions

1. Open setup and select **Booking site**, then **Rules**.
2. Enter your terms in **Terms and conditions**.
3. Tick **Require terms to be accepted** to make customers tick a box before they can complete a reservation.
4. Select **Save**.

## Terms for one service

To use other terms for one service or event type, edit it and fill in **Show custom terms**.

## If you use a custom reservation form

A custom form does not show the terms on its own. Add the terms text and the checkbox to the form yourself. See [Custom fields]({% link _services/custom-fields.md %}).

## The Terms link in the footer

The **Terms** link at the bottom of the booking site leads to MakePlans' own terms and privacy policy for the booking site. It cannot be removed.
