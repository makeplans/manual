# Cancellation by customers

Customers can cancel their own reservations on the booking site. On a new account they can do so until 24 hours before the reservation starts.

## Change the rules

1. Open setup and select **Booking site**, then **Rules**.
2. Tick or clear **Allow cancellations by customer**.
3. Set the **Cancellation period**, written in plain English and counted from the start of the reservation. For example, `2 days before` lets customers cancel until two days before. Leave it empty to allow cancellation right up to the start.
4. Select **Save**.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/booking-site/cancellation/01-cancellation-rules.webp" alt="Allow cancellations by customer ticked, with the cancellation period set to 24 hours before" %}

Reservations that are paid in advance cannot be cancelled by the customer.

## Different rules for one service or event

On a service, an event type or a single event you can override both settings. Set **Allow cancellations by customer** to **Enabled** or **Disabled** and enter a **Cancellation period**.

## How customers cancel

Customers open the reservation from their confirmation or from **My reservations** on the booking site and select **Cancel reservation**. You receive a notification when a customer cancels.
