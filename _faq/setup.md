# FAQ: Setup

## How do I block out vacation days?

Add a reservation of the type **Busy** that covers the whole period. For a single day, open the calendar, select the clock button and close the date. See [Vacations, holidays and blocking dates]({% link _availability/time-off.md %}).

## How do I add buffer time between appointments?

There is no separate buffer setting. Set **Available slot every** on the service to a longer time than its duration. A 45-minute service with a slot every 60 minutes leaves 15 minutes between appointments. See [Buffer time between appointments]({% link _availability/buffer-time.md %}).

## How do I stop customers booking at the last minute?

Open setup, select **Booking site**, then **Rules**, and fill in **Do not allow reservations until**, for example `in 24 hours`. See [Limit how far ahead customers can book]({% link _availability/booking-window.md %}).

## How do I create a free event?

Leave the price empty. Do not enter 0. See [Event types and events]({% link _services/events.md %}).

## Can you restore something I deleted by mistake?

Usually, yes.

- A deleted customer is only made inactive. If you create a customer with the same email or phone number, the old profile comes back.
- Deleted services, resources, events and coupons can often be restored. Contact support and tell us what was deleted.
- Deleting an event cancels its reservations. They have to be reactivated one by one.

## Can I block a customer from booking?

Yes. Open the customer, select **Edit** and use the block link in the menu on the left.

To let only people you have approved book, open setup, select **Booking site**, then **General**, and tick **Only allow reservations from existing customers**.

## Does the booking site adjust to the visitor's time zone?

No. All times are shown in the time zone of your account. If you have customers in several time zones, say which time zone applies in the footer of the booking site. See [Account settings]({% link _account/account-settings.md %}).

## Can customers see whether a time is free without booking?

Yes. Anyone can open your booking site and see the available times. They only identify themselves when they book.
