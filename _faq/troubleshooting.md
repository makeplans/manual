# FAQ: Troubleshooting

## My customer did not get the verification code

1. Check the phone number or email on the customer's profile. Codes go to what is stored there, not to what the customer types.
2. For email, ask the customer to check their spam folder.
3. A phone number from another country must have the country code.
4. A reservation that is not completed is released after about 20 minutes. The customer can book again to get a new code.

See [Troubleshooting SMS and email delivery]({% link _notifications/troubleshooting.md %}).

## The password reset email does not arrive

Check your spam folder, and that you used the email address you are registered with as a user. See [Your profile, password and login]({% link _account/login.md %}).

## How do I contact support?

Use the chat button in the corner of this page, or email [hello@makeplans.com](mailto:hello@makeplans.com). If you are a customer of a business that uses MakePlans, contact the business. We cannot change your reservation or your details for them.

## My data export never arrived

The email comes a few minutes after you start the export, and its link works for 15 minutes only. Check your spam folder and run the export again. See [Export and import data]({% link _account/export-import.md %}).

## What does "the values do not match your profile" mean?

The customer's email or phone number matches an existing profile, but something else, usually the name, is different. The customer can continue anyway. See [Customers]({% link _bookings/customers.md %}).

## No times are shown on my booking site

Check, in this order:

1. That a resource offering the service is open that day. See [Opening hours]({% link _availability/opening-hours.md %}).
2. That the service is offered by at least one resource.
3. That the date is not a public holiday, and is inside the period customers can book in. See [How available times are generated]({% link _availability/slot-generation.md %}).
4. If you use categories, that the service is in a category. See [Categories]({% link _services/categories.md %}).

## A service is missing from my booking site

Check that **Show on the booking site** is ticked on the service, that at least one resource offers it, and, if you use categories, that it is in one.
