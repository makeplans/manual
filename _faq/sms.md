# FAQ: SMS and messaging

## What does an SMS cost, and which messages count?

Every SMS sent from your account uses SMS credits: verification codes, confirmations, reminders, message templates, SMS you send yourself and SMS notifications to staff. One SMS is 160 characters, and a longer message counts as two or more. Email is free. See [SMS credits and senders]({% link _notifications/sms-and-senders.md %}).

## How do I change the SMS sender name?

With the add-on **Custom SMS sender ID**. The name can have at most 11 characters. See [SMS credits and senders]({% link _notifications/sms-and-senders.md %}).

## How do I turn off SMS?

Turn off SMS reminders and confirmations in setup under **Notifications - customer**. The verification SMS follows your verification method. See [Customer notifications]({% link _notifications/customer-notifications.md %}).

## Can I send an SMS or email to all my customers at once?

SMS, yes: open setup and select **Tools**, then **Send SMS to all customers**. There is no function for emailing all customers. Export your customers and use a newsletter service. See [Send an SMS to customers]({% link _notifications/send-sms.md %}).

## Can I send reminders by email?

Yes, with a message template of the type reminder. See [Message templates]({% link _notifications/message-templates.md %}).
