# FAQ: Account and billing

## Can you extend my free trial?

Yes. Contact us and we extend it. The trial is free and needs no payment card.

## What is the notice period when I cancel?

None. You are not billed again, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Amounts already paid are not refunded. See [Pause, cancel or reactivate]({% link _account/pause-cancel.md %}).

## Can I pause my account in the off-season?

Yes. Pause it in setup under **Billing**. A paused account costs nothing and keeps your settings and data. See [Pause, cancel or reactivate]({% link _account/pause-cancel.md %}).

## Can I pay by invoice or EHF instead of card?

Yes. Contact support and we turn it on. You are then billed for two months at a time. See [Invoices, receipts and paying]({% link _account/invoices.md %}).

## Where do I find my receipts and invoices?

A receipt with the invoice is emailed to your billing address after each payment. All invoices can be downloaded in setup under **Billing**.

## Do you have a discount for non-profits?

Yes, for non-profit and volunteer organisations. The size of the discount depends on how much you will use MakePlans, such as how many resources you need. Contact us with a short description. You do not need to send any papers. We trust that you are a non-profit.

## Why did my price change, or why does my invoice show quantity 2?

The price is per resource. Each invoice is based on the number of resources you have at that time. See [How billing works]({% link _account/billing.md %}).

## Do I pay per user?

No. Users are free. See [Users and access levels]({% link _account/users.md %}).
