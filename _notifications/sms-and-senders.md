# SMS credits and senders

## What uses SMS credits

Every SMS that is sent uses credits: verification codes, confirmations, reminders, message templates, SMS you send to customers yourself and SMS notifications to staff. Email is free.

One SMS is 160 characters. A longer message counts as two or more.

## Buy SMS credits

1. Open setup and select **SMS credits**. The page shows how many credits you have.
2. Select **Buy more SMS credits**.
3. Choose a pack and select **Purchase**.

Larger packs have a lower price per SMS. Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://makeplans.com/en/pricing/).

## Change the SMS sender

SMS are sent from "MakePlans", with your company name at the end of the message. With the add-on **Custom SMS sender ID**, SMS come from your own name instead.

Open setup and select **Addons**, then **Custom SMS sender ID**. The rules for the name:

- At most 11 characters.
- Letters A to Z, numbers, space and dash only.
- It must be the name of your brand or business.
- One sender name per account.

This is a paid add-on, and it is not available in every country. A phone number can also be used as sender, so that customers can reply.

## Change the email sender

Emails are sent from a MakePlans address, with your company name as the sender name and your account's email as the reply address. So when a customer replies, the reply goes to you.

With the add-on **Custom email sender**, emails are sent from an address on your own domain, such as `booking@yourcompany.com`. It requires changes to your domain's DNS records, and we recommend using a subdomain. Open setup and select **Addons**, then **Custom email sender**. This is a paid add-on.
