# Notifications

MakePlans sends messages to your customers and notifications to you and your staff. You can change which ones are sent, what they say, who sends them and who receives them.

## Messages to customers

| Message | When it is sent |
|---|---|
| Verification | When a customer confirms who they are on the booking site. A code by SMS or email, depending on the [verification method]({% link _booking_site/verification.md %}). |
| Confirmation | When a reservation is confirmed. By default it is sent the same way as the verification. |
| Reminder | Before the reservation. An SMS reminder is optional and off until you turn it on. |
| Modification and cancellation | When a reservation is changed or cancelled. |
| Awaiting confirmation and declined | When you [approve reservations manually]({% link _bookings/approve.md %}). |

## What it costs

Email is free. Every SMS that is sent uses SMS credits, whatever kind of message it is. See [SMS credits and senders]({% link _notifications/sms-and-senders.md %}).
