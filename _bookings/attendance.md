# Attendance status and no-shows

Each reservation can be given an attendance status as the appointment takes place. Using it is optional. It gives you an overview of the day and a record of who did not turn up.

## Set the status

1. Open the reservation, or select it in the calendar.
2. Select the status menu. It shows **Confirmed** until you choose something else.
3. Choose **Checked in**, **Started**, **Completed** or **No show**.

The agenda shows the status next to the service, for example "Physiotherapy consultation - Checked in".

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Checked in** | The customer has arrived. |
| **Started** | The appointment is under way. |
| **Completed** | The appointment is finished. |
| **No show** | The customer did not turn up. |

For events, select **Check in** on the event page to mark attendants as they arrive.

## Find reservations by status

In the **Agenda**, under **Filtering**, select an **Attendance status**.

## Keep track of no-shows

- The **No-shows** report shows no-shows per day, weekday, hour, resource and service, and the customers with the most no-shows. See [Reports]({% link _bookings/reports.md %}).
- To be warned about customers who often fail to turn up, open setup, select **UX** and enter a number in **Warn when a customer has this many no-shows**.
