# Reports

Open **Reports** in the main menu for figures on your reservations. The **Overview** gathers the most important numbers, and each report has its own tab.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/bookings/reports/01-overview.webp" alt="The reports overview with period and filter on the left and key figures, charts and lists on the right" %}

## Choose the period

On the left, under **Period**, choose **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This year** or **Custom**. Under **Ahead** you can look forward: **Next 7 days**, **Next 30 days** or **Next month**.

**Show per** switches the charts between days and weeks. Under **Filter**, choose a resource or a service and select **Show**.

The overview compares the period with the one before it.

## What is counted

- Reservation figures count confirmed reservations by the date they are reserved for.
- Revenue counts payments by the date they were processed, not the date of the reservation.

## The reports

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Confirmed reservations, revenue, cancellation rate and new customers, reservations per day, demand by day and time, booked value by service, reservations per resource and the latest reservations. **Needs attention** points out things to look at. |
| **Reservations** | Reservations and attendees per day, per weekday and per hour. |
| **Customers** | New and returning customers, top customers, and the rebooking rate: the share who made a new reservation within 8 weeks of their first visit in the period. |
| **Revenue** | Payments collected per day. |
| **Resources** | Reservations, attendees and booked value per resource. |
| **Services** | Reservations, attendees and booked value per service. |
| **Utilization** | Booked hours against open hours per resource. |
| **No-shows** | No-shows per day, weekday, hour, resource and service, and the customers with the most no-shows. |
| **Ratings** | Average rating per day, resource and service, the response rate and the lowest rated reservations. |
| **Events** | Each event with attendees, capacity, fill rate and waitlist, and how early events fill up. |
| **Timing** | How far ahead customers book, when reservations are made, and how late cancellations come. |

## More detailed reporting

For your own calculations, export the data and work with it in a spreadsheet. Open setup and select **Export**. For payment reporting, use the reports of your payment provider, since that is where the money is processed.
