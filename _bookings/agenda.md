# The agenda

The **Agenda** lists your reservations and events in time order. It is the quickest way to see what is coming up and to find a particular reservation.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/bookings/agenda/01-agenda-week.webp" alt="The agenda for one week, with the period and filters on the left and the reservations per day on the right" %}

Each line shows the time, the resource, the service and the customer. A customer who has not been in before is marked "New customer". Select a line to open the reservation.

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Reservations in time order. |
| **Awaiting confirmation** | Reservations waiting for your approval, with the number waiting. The tab is only shown when there are any. See [Approve reservations manually]({% link _bookings/approve.md %}). |
| **Latest changes** | The reservations that were changed most recently. |
| **New reservations** | The reservations that were made most recently. |

## Period

Under **Period**, choose **List**, **Day**, **Week** or **Month**. Use the small calendar to jump to a date, and the arrows above the list to go back and forward.

## Filtering

Under **Filtering** you can narrow the list:

- **Resources** and **Services**
- **Attendance status**: Checked in, Started, Completed, No show
- **State**: Awaiting confirmation, Awaiting payment, Waitlisted, Declined, Cancelled

Cancelled and declined reservations are not shown until you select their state. Select **Reset filter** to clear the filters.
