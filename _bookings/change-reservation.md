# Change, move or cancel a reservation

Open the reservation from the **Calendar** or the **Agenda**. Its page shows the resource, the service, the time, the status and the customer, with a log of what has happened to it.

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/bookings/reservation/01-reservation-page.webp" alt="A reservation page with the status menu open, Cancel reservation and Show in calendar" %}

## Change a reservation

1. Select **Edit**.
2. Change the service, resource, date or time. The free times that day are listed under **Available slots** on the left.
3. Under **Modification**, tick **Email** or **SMS** to tell the customer about the change. Under **Notification**, choose whether the resource is told.
4. Select **Save appointment**.

You can also move a reservation by dragging it to another time in the calendar.

## Cancel a reservation

1. Select **Cancel reservation**.
2. Confirm that you want to cancel.

The status changes to Cancelled and the time becomes free again. The customer is sent a cancellation message, and you receive a notification about it.

Cancelling is the preferred way to remove a reservation. The reservation is kept and shown as cancelled. To see cancelled reservations, filter the [agenda]({% link _bookings/agenda.md %}) on the state **Cancelled**, or open the customer and select **Show all reservations including deleted**.

## Delete a reservation

Deleting removes every record of the reservation. It is no longer shown on the customer's page or anywhere else. Use it only for a reservation that should never have existed, such as a test or a mistake.

To delete, select **Edit**, then **Delete**.

## The log

The **Log** on the reservation page lists each step with date and time, such as when the reservation was confirmed or cancelled, and by whom.

## Which messages are sent

Whether customers are messaged when you create, change or cancel a reservation is set under **Notifications - customer**. See [Notifications]({% link _notifications/overview.md %}).
