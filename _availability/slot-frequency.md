# Slot frequency

By default a new slot starts when the previous one ends. A 60-minute service on a resource open 09:00 to 14:00 gives five slots: 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00 and 13:00.

To give customers more start times to choose from, set how often a slot starts.

1. Open setup, select **Services** and edit the service.
2. Enter the number of minutes in **Available slot every**.
3. Select **Save service**.

With **Available slot every** set to 30, the same service gives nine slots: 09:00, 09:30, 10:00 and so on until 13:00.

Leave the field empty to go back to one slot per duration.

## Watch out for gaps

More start times can leave gaps that are too short to book. With a 60-minute service and reservations at 09:30 to 10:30 and 11:00 to 12:30, the times 09:00 to 09:30 and 10:30 to 11:00 are free but cannot be booked.
