# How billing works

MakePlans is paid in advance and has no lock-in period.

## What you pay for

- **Resources.** The price is per resource. Each invoice is based on the number of resources you have when it is made. Add or delete a resource, and the next invoice changes. Users are free.
- **SMS.** Each SMS you send uses credits. See [SMS credits and senders]({% link _notifications/sms-and-senders.md %}).
- **Add-ons**, if you use any. See [Add-ons]({% link _account/addons.md %}).

Taking payments from customers has its own fees. See [Fees, refunds and no-shows]({% link _payments/fees-and-refunds.md %}).

Current prices are on the [pricing page](https://makeplans.com/en/pricing/).

## When you are charged

Your card is charged automatically each month, for the month ahead. If you would like to pay yearly, or by invoice, contact us. See [Invoices, receipts and paying]({% link _account/invoices.md %}).

## Activate your account

A new account is a free trial. No card is needed. To continue after the trial:

1. Open setup and select **Billing**.
2. Select **Activate account - Enter credit card details**.
3. Enter your card details, then your company details for the invoices.

The page shows what you will be charged per month based on your current use.

Need more time to try things out? Contact us and we will extend the trial.

## Why your invoice shows quantity 2

Two resources means the price per resource with quantity 2. If you only run events, one resource is usually enough. See [How many resources do I need]({% link _availability/how-many-resources.md %}).

## Non-profit organisations

We give a discount to non-profit and volunteer organisations. Contact us with a short description of how you will use MakePlans.
