# GDPR and privacy

MakePlans is a Norwegian company and follows the GDPR. You are the controller of your customers' data, and MakePlans processes it for you.

## Data processing agreement

The data processing agreement (DPA) is part of the [terms of service](https://makeplans.com/en/tos/), which you accept when you register. You do not need to sign anything separately. Enterprise customers can get their own DPA and SLA.

## Where data is stored

All data is encrypted, both when stored and when sent. It is hosted in Norway, in a data centre certified to ISO 27001. More details are on our [compliance page](https://makeplans.com/en/compliance/).

## A customer asks for their data

1. Open **Customers** and select the customer.
2. Select **Email export of data to customer**.

The customer receives an email with their data.

## A customer asks to be deleted

Open the customer, select **Edit**, then **Delete**.

## Do not keep data longer than needed

Set up automatic deletion of old customers and reservations. See [Delete old data]({% link _account/delete-old-data.md %}).

## Your own copy

You can export all your data at any time. See [Export and import data]({% link _account/export-import.md %}).
