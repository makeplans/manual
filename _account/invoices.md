# Invoices, receipts and paying

## Receipts and invoices

Each time your card is charged, a receipt with the invoice attached is emailed to your billing address. You can also download all your invoices in setup under **Billing**.

Missing an invoice? Contact us and we will send it again.

## Change your invoice details

In setup under **Billing** you can change:

- company name and address
- organisation number
- billing email
- an invoice reference

If your VAT number must be on the invoice, put it in the invoice reference.

## Pay by invoice or EHF

| Method | For |
|---|---|
| EHF (e-invoice) | Norwegian organisations that can receive EHF. SMS use is invoiced the same way. |
| Invoice by email | Paid by bank transfer. |

Contact support to change from card to invoice. We have to turn it on for you. With invoice, you are billed for two months at a time.

## A card payment failed

You receive an email saying what went wrong, for example an expired card. We try again daily for several days.

To fix it, update your card in setup under **Billing**. If the payment still cannot be made, the account is deactivated automatically. Nothing is lost, and you can reactivate it when the payment is sorted out.

## Your bank asks you to confirm the payment

Sometimes your bank wants you to approve a card payment yourself, in Norway usually with BankID. This follows from EU rules on strong customer authentication. Your bank decides when, not MakePlans. Follow the link in the email and approve the payment.

To avoid this, change to invoice or EHF.
