# Pause, cancel or reactivate

There is no notice period and no binding contract. When you stop, you are not billed again, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Amounts already paid are not refunded.

## Pause or cancel

You do this yourself while logged in. For security, we cannot pause or cancel an account on the strength of an email.

1. Open setup and select **Billing**.
2. Select **Pause the account when the current subscription ends**.

When the paid period ends, the account is deactivated. You get no more invoices, and you cannot log in or receive reservations until you reactivate.

## What a paused account keeps

A paused account costs nothing. Your settings, services, resources, customers and reservation history are all kept until you come back. This suits businesses that close for a season.

Before you pause:

- Close the booking site so customers do not try to book. Open setup, select **Booking site**, then **General**, and clear **The booking site is available to the public**.
- If you want a copy of your data, export it in setup under **Export**.

## Reactivate

Log in and reactivate from the **Billing** page. Everything is as you left it, and billing starts again from that day.

- You need at least one resource to reactivate. If you deleted your resources, add a new one.
- If you closed the booking site, open it again under **Booking site**, then **General**.

## Pause or delete?

Pausing keeps everything. Deleting is permanent and removes the account and all its data. See [Delete your account]({% link _account/delete-account.md %}).

An account that stays deactivated for a long time may be removed when old accounts are cleaned up. Export your data first if it matters to you.
