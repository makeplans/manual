# Add-ons: your own domain and senders

Open setup and select **Addons**. Each add-on has a monthly price, shown on its page and on the [pricing page](https://makeplans.com/en/pricing/).

| Add-on | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Custom domain** | Your booking site on your own address, such as `booking.example.com`, instead of `youraccount.makeplans.com`. |
| **Custom SMS sender ID** | SMS sent from your own name instead of "MakePlans". |
| **Custom email sender** | Emails sent from an address on your own domain. |

## Custom domain

1. Open setup and select **Addons**, then **Custom domain**.
2. Select **Add your domain** and enter the domain.
3. Add the DNS records you are given at your domain provider.

Use a subdomain of your website, such as `booking.example.com`, not the main domain.
